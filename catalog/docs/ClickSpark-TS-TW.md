# ClickSpark
**Registry**: `ClickSpark-TS-TW` · **Deps**: none · **Files**: ClickSpark/ClickSpark.tsx
**What it does** — Generates a radial burst of animated particles from the cursor position whenever a user clicks anywhere inside its container. Particles can take the form of lines, streaks, dots, or four-point stars that decelerate, fall with gravity, and fade out.
**Props** — `sparkColor` (string | string[], '#ffffff') — particle color or array of colors; `sparkCount` (number, 8) — number of sparks emitted per click; `sparkSize` (number, 10) — particle length or diameter in pixels; `sparkRadius` (number, 15) — maximum radial distance traveled; `duration` (number, 400) — particle lifetime in milliseconds; `shape` ('line' | 'streak' | 'dot' | 'star', 'line') — geometric particle shape; `gravity` (number, 0) — downward acceleration pulling particles; `easing` ('linear' | 'ease-in' | 'ease-out' | 'ease-in-out' | 'spring', 'ease-out') — velocity falloff curve.
**Motion** — Imperative 2D canvas rAF loop triggered on click, updating position, length, and alpha per particle over the specified duration. Under reduced motion, spark trajectories are suppressed in favor of a brief stationary fading ring. No external animation libraries.
**Cost** — Zero dependencies, ~16 KB source; mounts a full-bleed absolute `<canvas>` element (pointer-events-none); canvas rAF loop executes only while bursts are active. Fully safe for static prerendering (empty canvas).
**Fit for ARKRETE** — no fit. Arkrete is an austere, dark luxury fit-out contractor where photography is the argument; celebratory click sparks or star bursts demean the solemn architectural tone.
**Verdict** — skip — celebratory particle sparks upon clicking conflict with Arkrete's dark luxury discipline and austere architectural tone.
