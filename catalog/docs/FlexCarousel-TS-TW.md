# FlexCarousel
**Registry**: `FlexCarousel-TS-TW` · **Deps**: ogl@^1.0.11 · **Files**: FlexCarousel/FlexCarousel.tsx
**What it does** — Draws an endless horizontal row of photographs in WebGL, bent through a glass lens at the centre so cards swell and refract as they pass; drag, wheel or arrow through them, and click one to open it full-frame. Titles sit in the DOM under the row, and a small counter ticks over.
**Props** — `items` ({ src, alt, title, subtitle }[], twelve Unsplash defaults) — the photographs; `preset` ('liquid' | 'ribbon' | 'vortex' | 'arch', 'liquid') — the lens character; `cardHeight` (number, 0.5) — card height as a fraction of the container; `gap` (number, 12) — pixels between cards; `captions` (boolean, true) — DOM title and subtitle under the row; `autoplay` (boolean, false) — advance on a timer; `focusOnClick` (boolean, true) — click opens the card full-frame; `onChange` (index, item) — fires as the active card changes.
**Motion** — A spring-damped scroll position on rAF, with drag inertia and wheel capture; a second shader pass bends and disperses each card through the lens, with an intro of rise, bloom, spin or deal. Reduced motion swaps the intro for a fade and switches off autoplay, liquid warp and card shift.
**Cost** — 46 KB of source plus ogl (roughly 10–15 KB gzip), a texture per image and a render target per frame; twelve 1200 px photographs is tens of megabytes of VRAM. Everything visible is canvas, so images, titles and the counter are absent from the prerendered HTML until JS runs, and the default items are hot-linked Unsplash URLs that would have to be replaced.
**Fit for ARKRETE** — no fit — projects is a vertical stack and home is a scroll reel; a horizontal lens row has no slot, and the lens warps the photography that is supposed to be the argument.
**Verdict** — skip — 46 KB of WebGL that distorts the one asset the site exists to show.
