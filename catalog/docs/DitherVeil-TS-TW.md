# DitherVeil
**Registry**: `DitherVeil-TS-TW` · **Deps**: `ogl@^1.0.11` · **Files**: `DitherVeil/DitherVeil.tsx`
**What it does** — A photograph is shown as a one-bit duotone dither — ink on paper, Floyd–Steinberg or Atkinson — and a soft circular mask burns through it to the full-colour image under the cursor. The colour lingers behind the pointer and the dither knits back cell by cell.
**Props** — `src` (string, unsplash default) — the photo · `pattern` (`'floyd' | 'atkinson' | 'bayer' | 'noise' | 'lines'`, `'floyd'`) — dither algorithm · `pixelSize` (number, `2`) — cell size · `levels` (number, `2`) — tone steps · `palette` (`'duotone' | 'rgb'`, `'duotone'`) — ink/paper or colour · `inkColor` / `paperColor` (string, `'#120f17'` / `'#f4f1ea'`) — the duotone ends · `revealRadius` (number, `200`) — brush size · `linger` (number, `1`) — seconds the colour holds behind the pointer.
**Motion** — A WebGL mask pass accumulates a fading trail, and the view shader mixes dithered and full-colour sampling by that mask, with an optional rim and click bursts. `wander` moves the reveal on its own when the pointer leaves. The rAF loop runs only while the pointer is active, a burst is alive, or the intro is playing.
**Cost** — `ogl@^1.0.11` (roughly 20 KB gzip) plus 26.3 KB of source (8.2 KB gzip). Floyd/Atkinson error diffusion runs synchronously on a 2D canvas with `getImageData` whenever the cell size, cover or grading changes — that is the expensive part, not the shader. DPR capped at 2; canvas is injected into a div, so it is client-only.
**Fit for ARKRETE** — Projects rooms could carry it, but it shows every room as a dither until touched, which undersells the photography the page exists to display.
**Verdict** — skip — ogl plus a CPU diffusion pass to make the rooms look worse than the photographs.
