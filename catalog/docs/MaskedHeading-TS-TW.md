# MaskedHeading
**Registry**: `MaskedHeading-TS-TW` · **Deps**: `gsap@^3.13.0` · **Files**: MaskedHeading/MaskedHeading.tsx
**What it does** — Renders a large typographic heading whose letter glyphs serve as an SVG clipping mask over an underlying photo or looping video. As the heading scrolls or hovers into view, GSAP reveals the words glyph-by-glyph while the inner media drifts with mouse parallax and ambient motion.
**Props** — `text` (string, 'Designed in the details') — headline copy parsed into SVG glyph mask paths; `src` (string, '') — URL of the image or video clipped inside the letterforms; `mediaType` ('image' | 'video', 'image') — asset format used for the masked media background; `reveal` ('rise' | 'scale' | 'blur' | 'wipe' | 'fade' | 'none', 'rise') — entrance transition choreographing glyphs; `duration` / `stagger` (number, 1.1 / 0.09) — GSAP animation timing and stagger delay between letters; `parallax` / `drift` (number, 26 / 18) — mouse displacement scale and ambient idle wander distance; `spotlight` (boolean, false) — whether a cursor-following radial spotlight illuminates the fill; `outline` / `outlineColor` (boolean / string, false / '') — stroke geometry around individual glyphs.
**Motion** — GSAP timeline animates SVG text glyph transforms (`translate`, `scale`, `opacity`, `blur`) on trigger. Pointer move updates CSS transform matrix for parallax and coordinates for the radial mask. An active rAF loop drives ambient sinusoidal media drift. Requires `gsap` core. Reads `prefers-reduced-motion` in two places: the reveal timeline is skipped and the glyphs settle at once, and an autoplaying video is paused.
**Cost** — 703 lines (~23 KB source) plus GSAP (~25 KB gzip). Computes DOM glyph boundaries via `getBoundingClientRect` on resize, recalculating SVG transforms on the main thread alongside a continuous rAF loop. Prerenders static fallback typography via `useIsomorphicLayoutEffect`, but client hydration triggers heavy layout recalculation.
**Fit for ARKRETE** — no fit. ARKRETE treats interior photography as the argument, displaying full-bleed spatial views; cutting room photography into letterform silhouettes reduces architectural photography to graphic fill texture, and home's monument wordmark is already an intentional drawn SVG.
**Verdict** — skip — clipping room photography into letterforms degrades architectural images and pulls in GSAP for decorative typography.
