# FoldText
**Registry**: `FoldText-TS-TW` · **Deps**: `gsap@^3.13.0` · **Files**: `FoldText/FoldText.tsx`
**What it does** — Characters, words, or lines rotate into view in 3D along a top, bottom, or side hinge like origami paper creases unfolding flat or louvre blinds snapping shut. As each segment opens, a dynamic gradient crease shadow fades away across the fold line.
**Props** — `text` (string, 'Design unfolds') — string to segment and animate; `splitBy` ('char' | 'word' | 'line', 'char') — granularity of text segmentation; `hinge` ('top' | 'bottom' | 'left' | 'right', 'top') — rotation axis and edge pivot; `foldStyle` ('paper' | 'blinds' | 'flip', 'paper') — single-crease origami, dual-slat louvre, or 3D card flip; `duration` (number, 0.65) — unfolding duration per piece in seconds; `stagger` (number, 0.045) — delay between successive segment animations; `foldAngle` (number, 92) — starting 3D rotation angle in degrees; `trigger` ('mount' | 'hover' | 'scroll' | 'loop', 'mount') — animation trigger.
**Motion** — GSAP timeline and ScrollTrigger animating CSS 3D transforms (`rotateX`/`rotateY`) and opacity on split spans; optional pointer-proximity refolding loop. Respects `prefers-reduced-motion` cleanly by disabling 3D rotation and crease shadows.
**Cost** — 14.9 KB source + `gsap` (~60 KB minified) + `ScrollTrigger`. Splits text into dozens of nested span elements. Pre-renders spans safely via isomorphic layout effect, but hides text with `opacity: 0` before mount if trigger is not 'hover'.
**Fit for ARKRETE** — no fit — folding origami paper or louvre blinds typography reads as craft-paper novelty rather than solid stone and architectural woodwork.
**Verdict** — skip — paper-crease text animation feels crafty rather than architectural and adds 60 KB GSAP for novelty typography.
