# MicroSlats
**Registry**: `MicroSlats-TS-TW` · **Deps**: `ogl@^1.0.11` · **Files**: MicroSlats/MicroSlats.tsx
**What it does** — Renders a dense geometric field of rounded miniature slats in perspective that undulate like an ocean swell with glinting highlights. Driven by an underlying multi-pass Navier-Stokes fluid simulation (velocity advection, vorticity curl, divergence, 16-step pressure Poisson solver) interacting with cursor drags.
**Props** — `preset` ('swell' | 'tide' | 'storm' | 'signal', 'swell') — wave motion profile preset configuring scale, speed, chop, and fog; `color` (string, '#A855F7') — base slat surface color; `glintColor` (string, '#ffffff') — specular highlight color catching the wave crests; `slatWidth` / `slatHeight` (number, 10 / 25) — individual slat dimensions in pixels; `gap` (number, 3) — spacing between slats in the perspective grid; `speed` (number, from preset) — wave propagation rate across the grid; `interactive` (boolean, true) — whether mouse dragging injects velocity into the fluid solver; `cursorStrength` (number, 1) — magnitude of mouse-induced fluid disturbance.
**Motion** — WebGL via OGL. Runs 7+ render passes per frame (splat, curl, vorticity, divergence, 16 Jacobi pressure iterations, advection, and final scene composite) on requestAnimationFrame. Mouse movements and drags inject fluid impulse splats. No GSAP or Framer Motion. Reads `prefers-reduced-motion`: the wave clock is frozen, the intro jumps to its end, and the pointer and drag splats are disabled.
**Cost** — 1,003 lines (~33 KB source) plus `ogl` (~25 KB gzip). Severe GPU compute load running iterative ping-pong texture solvers and framebuffers every frame. Client-only: mounts OGL canvas inside useEffect; SSR prerender outputs empty container.
**Fit for ARKRETE** — no fit. A rolling synthetic sea of purple slats running fluid dynamic equations is purely decorative digital tech art with zero connection to architectural interior fit-outs, while placing heavy demands on mobile GPU and battery budgets.
**Verdict** — skip — heavy multi-pass OGL fluid simulation and rolling slat sea that adds unnecessary GPU overhead and clashes with quiet architecture.
