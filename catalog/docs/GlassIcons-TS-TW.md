# GlassIcons
**Registry**: `GlassIcons-TS-TW` · **Deps**: none · **Files**: GlassIcons/GlassIcons.tsx
**What it does** — A grid of square "liquid glass" icon tiles, each floating over a coloured plate it bends and tints, with an icon centred on top and an optional label that appears on hover. Tiles can tilt, lift, float or press, and the plate can sit behind at an angle or be removed for a clear-glass look.
**Props** — `items` (GlassIconsItem[], []) — icon, label, colour, href or onClick per tile; `size` (number, 82) — tile edge in px; `columns` (number, 3) — grid columns; `refraction` (number, 0.8) — how hard the glass bends the plate; `dispersion` (number, 0) — chromatic split at the edge; `plate` ('tilt' | 'offset' | 'circle' | 'none', 'tilt') — coloured shape behind the glass; `hover` ('lift' | 'float' | 'press' | 'none', 'press') — pointer response; `labels` ('hover' | 'always' | 'none', 'hover') — when captions show.
**Motion** — Springs on tilt, lift, scale and label fade, plus a staggered intro; pointer position aims the refraction highlight. One rAF loop runs only while something is settling. Reduced motion snaps every spring to its target, so the grid is still but usable.
**Cost** — 41.4 KB, no packages. Each tile is roughly six spans with `backdrop-filter`, mask images and a shine overlay; drawing goes through a WebGL2 canvas layer when available, otherwise three `feDisplacementMap`s, three `feColorMatrix`es, two blends and a blur per tile. Client-only for the GPU layer; the SVG path is expensive on wide grids.
**Fit for ARKRETE** — No fit. The contact page's phone and email links should be two lines of text; an app-icon dock belongs to a product, and the coloured plates would add four accents the palette does not have.
**Verdict** — skip — 41 KB and per-tile filters to render a link row that plain type does better.
