# Plasma
**Registry**: `Plasma-TS-TW` · **Deps**: none · **Files**: `Plasma/Plasma.tsx`
**What it does** — A full-bleed WebGL2 background of a twisting, glowing volumetric plasma fluid that writhes in 3D space. The camera subtly tilts and orbits with pointer movement, leaving localized thermal glow spots across the fluid field.
**Props** — `color` (string, '#a6a3b8') — base tint of the volumetric plasma tendrils; `speed` (number, 1) — rate of temporal wave oscillation and twisting motion; `direction` ('forward' | 'reverse' | 'pingpong', 'forward') — timeline direction of fluid evolution; `scale` / `twist` / `wave` (number, 1 / 1 / 1) — spatial frequency, rotational twisting, and lateral undulation; `softness` / `shine` (number, 0.5 / 1) — edge falloff diffusion and specular highlight power; `brightness` / `grain` (number, 1 / 0.04) — overall exposure level and film grain noise amplitude; `quality` (number, 0.75) — render target resolution divisor for raymarching performance; `mouseInteractive` (boolean, true) — enables 3D camera orbit rotation and pointer heat spots.
**Motion** — Dual-pass WebGL2 raymarching pipeline: a volume shader marches 60 steps per pixel through an implicit mathematical surface, followed by a composite shader applying tonemapping, specular response, and dither grain. Continuous time drives animation. Pauses when scrolled out of view and freezes on prefers-reduced-motion.
**Cost** — 18.5 KB source (~3.8 KB gzip), zero dependencies. Single `<canvas>` DOM element. Very heavy GPU workload: 60 raymarching steps per pixel every frame, stressing integrated GPUs. Client-only; blank on static prerender.
**Fit for ARKRETE** — No fit. A sci-fi glowing plasma cloud is completely antithetical to physical fit-out craft and real architectural finishes like stone, oak, and brass.
**Verdict** — skip — heavy 60-step volumetric raymarching shader with sci-fi aesthetics alien to physical fit-out craft.
