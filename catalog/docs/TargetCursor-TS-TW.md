# TargetCursor
**Registry**: `TargetCursor-TS-TW` · **Deps**: `react-dom@^19.0.0` · **Files**: `TargetCursor/TargetCursor.tsx`
**What it does** — Replaces the pointer with four corner brackets and a centre dot that spring after the mouse, rotate slowly at rest and snap around whatever matches `targetSelector`. The brackets stretch to the target's box, inherit its corner radius, can read a `data-cursor-label`, and squeeze on click.
**Props** — `targetSelector` (string, `.cursor-target`) — what it locks onto; `cursorColor` / `cursorColorOnTarget` (string, #ffffff) — bracket colour, locked and unlocked; `spinDuration` (number, 2) — seconds per idle revolution; `hoverDuration` (number, 0.2) — snap speed; `size` (number, 36), `cornerSize` (number, 12), `thickness` (number, 3) — bracket geometry; `padding` (number, 6) — gap around a locked target; `matchRadius` (boolean, true) — copy the target's border radius; `blendMode` (CSS mix-blend-mode, difference) — how it sits on photography.
**Motion** — A raf loop integrates damped springs (centre, half-width and half-height, corner radius, rotation, lock, press, presence) and writes transforms to four spans and a dot; it spins continuously when idle and rotates to the nearest quarter-turn when it locks. Reduced motion kills the idle spin but keeps the tracking. Needs only `react-dom` for the portal.
**Cost** — No animation library, ~14 KB source, one fixed overlay portalled to `document.body` plus an injected `<style>` that applies `cursor: none` to the scope. Fine-pointer only — it returns null on touch, so no markup is prerendered; the overlay sits at `z-index: 2147483647` and repaints on every frame the pointer moves.
**Fit for ARKRETE** — no fit. Hiding the native cursor taxes every click on a site whose only job is to show rooms, and a reticle reads as a weapon sight, not a fit-out.
**Verdict** — skip — a custom cursor is usability debt, and the bracket-and-label look is game HUD, not brass-and-bone.
