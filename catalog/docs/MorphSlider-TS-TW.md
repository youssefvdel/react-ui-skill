# MorphSlider
**Registry**: `MorphSlider-TS-TW` · **Deps**: `ogl@^1.0.11`, `gsap@^3.13.0` · **Files**: MorphSlider/MorphSlider.tsx
**What it does** — Transitions between gallery images using WebGL displacement shaders (melt, ripple, shear, swirl) combined with chromatic aberration and ambient drift. GSAP drives the transition progress uniform while OGL renders the distorted cross-fading textures onto a canvas quad.
**Props** — `items` (MorphItem[], DEFAULT_ITEMS) — array of slide objects containing `image` and `caption`; `transition` ('melt' | 'ripple' | 'shear' | 'swirl', 'melt') — shader distortion effect during slide switch; `duration` (number, 1.1) — transition duration in seconds driven by GSAP tween; `intensity` (number, 0.55) — strength of shader warp displacement and swirl; `aberration` (number, 0.35) — RGB channel split separation during transition; `drift` (number, 0.4) — subtle continuous idle zoom and float oscillation; `autoplay` (boolean, false) — whether slides advance automatically after autoplayDelay; `showControls` / `showIndicators` (boolean, true) — displays prev/next buttons and dot indicators.
**Motion** — WebGL via OGL combined with GSAP. GSAP tweens `uProgress` from 0 to 1 with `power2.inOut` easing when changing slides; continuous rAF updates ambient drift and pointer uniforms. When `prefers-reduced-motion` is active (`uReduce`), the shader bypasses distortion and performs a plain crossfade.
**Cost** — 793 lines (~25.5 KB source). Heavy dependencies: `ogl` (~25 KB gzip) plus `gsap` (~25 KB gzip) = ~50 KB gzip total (a third of the 150 KB initial JS budget). GPU texture swapping and multi-sampling. Client-only: SSR renders empty container; WebGL canvas attaches in useEffect.
**Fit for ARKRETE** — no fit. ARKRETE's presentation relies on a "scroll-driven reel of six rooms" on home and a "scroll stack of rooms" on projects; melting, rippling, or swirling interior architectural photography like liquid plastic distorts and cheapens the craft of the rooms, and costs 50 KB gzip in libraries for a slider.
**Verdict** — skip — melting/rippling WebGL distortion deforms architectural photography and costs 50 KB gzip in OGL + GSAP.
