# BlurText
**Registry**: `BlurText-TS-TW` · **Deps**: motion@^12.23.12 · **Files**: BlurText/BlurText.tsx
**What it does** — A line of text starts out of focus and displaced, then resolves word by word or letter by letter into sharp type. Five flavours: a plain blur, a zoom from 1.4×, a 3D tilt, a focus pull from 0.32 opacity, and a skew drift in from the side.
**Props** — `text` (string, required) — the copy to reveal; `animateBy` ('words' | 'letters', 'words') — unit that staggers; `effect` ('blur' | 'zoom' | 'tilt' | 'focus' | 'drift', 'blur') — entrance look; `trigger` ('view' | 'load' | 'hover' | 'click', 'view') — what starts it; `delay` (number, 200) — ms between pieces; `blur` (number, 10) — starting blur in px; `distance` (number, 50) — starting travel in px; `stepDuration` (number, 0.35) — seconds of each keyframe step.
**Motion** — Framer Motion keyframes per piece, with a spring on transform and an eased filter; an IntersectionObserver at `threshold` 0.1 fires the `view` trigger once, `loop` replays it after `loopDelay`, and `staggerFrom` orders the pieces from start, centre, end or a seeded random. Transforms and filters only. `MotionConfig reducedMotion="user"` is set, so the entrance collapses to an instant state change for those who ask.
**Cost** — 4 KB gzipped of source plus the Motion runtime (about 30 KB gzipped if the shell does not already ship it); one span per word, nested spans per letter, every piece carrying `will-change` and a blur filter during the entrance. Because the initial snapshot is inline, prerendered HTML for `trigger="view"` is blurred and transparent — with JS blocked or slow the heading stays invisible. Put it below the first viewport only.
**Fit for ARKRETE** — Services, the five trade headings; About, the opening line of the firm paragraph — one reveal per viewport, never the hero.
**Verdict** — adapt — keep only the plain `blur` effect at about 6px and 16px of travel, fire once on view, and drop `loop`, `focusOnHover` and the zoom/tilt/drift theatre.
