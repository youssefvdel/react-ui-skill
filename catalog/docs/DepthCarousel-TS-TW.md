# DepthCarousel
**Registry**: `DepthCarousel-TS-TW` · **Deps**: `gsap@^3.13.0` · **Files**: `DepthCarousel/DepthCarousel.tsx`
**What it does** — A rail of photo cards in 3D: the front card is sharp and full size, and the ones behind step backwards, slide sideways, dim and blur. Drag, wheel or arrow keys move the rail, and it settles on whichever card is nearest.
**Props** — `items` (string[] or `{ image, alt }[]`, six picsum defaults) — the photos · `cardWidth` / `cardHeight` (number, `300` / `380`) — card box · `depth` (number, `220`) — px each card recedes · `spread` (number, `90`) — px each card slides sideways · `tilt` (number, `22`) — rotateY on the cards behind · `visibleCards` (number, `4`) — how many stay in view · `blur` (number, `6`) — blur on the back cards · `autoplay` (boolean, `false`) / `autoplayDelay` (number, `3200`) — unattended advance.
**Motion** — One GSAP tween animates a float position, and every card's transform, opacity, filter and z-index is rewritten on each frame of that tween; release velocity from a drag is projected into the snap target. Wheel input scrubs the rail and snaps 130 ms after the last event. Autoplay pauses on hover and focus; reduced motion collapses every tween to zero duration.
**Cost** — Pulls in `gsap@^3.13.0` (roughly 25 KB gzip for core) to run one rail. 16 KB source (4.6 KB gzip); every card and every image is mounted and loaded eagerly, with no lazy loading. The wheel listener is non-passive and calls `preventDefault`, so the page cannot be scrolled while the pointer is over it.
**Fit for ARKRETE** — Projects, where the brief already calls for a scroll stack of rooms; this would be a second, competing way to move through the same images and would trap the scroll the page depends on.
**Verdict** — skip — GSAP for a rail that duplicates the room stack and blocks page scroll.
