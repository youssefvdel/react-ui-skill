# ScrollFloat
**Registry**: `ScrollFloat-TS-TW` · **Deps**: gsap@^3.13.0 · **Files**: ScrollFloat/ScrollFloat.tsx
**What it does** — Splits a heading into words or characters and floats them into place as the heading crosses the viewport, scrubbed to scroll rather than fired once. Four louder variants flip, scatter, blur or wave the letters, with an optional shift from a start colour to the current one.
**Props** — `effect` ('float' | 'flip' | 'scatter' | 'blur' | 'wave', float) — entry motion; `splitBy` ('chars' | 'words', chars) — unit that moves; `direction` ('up' | 'down', up) — travel direction; `order` ('start' | 'end' | 'center' | 'edges' | 'random', start) — stagger order; `stagger` (0.03s) — delay per unit; `distance` (120) — travel in px; `stretch` (2.3) — vertical squash at the start; `smoothing` (0) — scrub lag.
**Motion** — A GSAP timeline is scrubbed by ScrollTrigger across the heading's passage (default start 'center bottom+=50%', end 'bottom bottom-=40%'), so the letters track the scrollbar: opacity, yPercent, scaleX/scaleY and optional per-character colour-mix. `gsap.matchMedia` gates the whole thing to no-preference; reduced motion leaves the heading as plain text. Needs GSAP and ScrollTrigger.
**Cost** — GSAP core plus ScrollTrigger, roughly 35 KB gzip together, and one span per character — a long heading is 40+ nodes carrying will-change. One ScrollTrigger per heading, re-measured on refresh; no rAF while idle. Client-only behaviour, but it prerenders the full heading, so nothing is hidden before hydration.
**Fit for ARKRETE** — services, the five trade headings, or the about statement. It could be tuned to a 12 px rise with 0.02 stagger, but the presets that justify the library — scatter, flip, wave on 2.3× vertical stretch — are exactly the loud ones the brand cannot use.
**Verdict** — skip — GSAP plus ScrollTrigger for a per-character rise that fifteen lines of IntersectionObserver and a CSS transition already do calmly.
