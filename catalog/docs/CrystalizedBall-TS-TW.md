# CrystalizedBall
**Registry**: `CrystalizedBall-TS-TW` · **Deps**: ogl@^1.0.11 · **Files**: `CrystalizedBall/CrystalizedBall.tsx`
**What it does** — A single glass sphere in WebGL: a crackling electric rim, coloured strands turning inside it, and twelve to twenty thousand dust particles that swirl toward the cursor. Eight presets (plasma, aurora, nebula, ember, frost, solar, eclipse, abyss) change palette, fill and particle behaviour.
**Props** — `preset` ('plasma' … 'abyss', 'plasma') — the whole look. `size` (number, 0.7; 0–1.5) — sphere diameter as a fraction of the box. `strands` / `crackle` / `flares` / `sparks` / `glow` — rim and interior character. `particleCount` (number, 12k–20k) — dust density. `motion` ('rise' | 'fall' | 'drift' | 'orbit') — dust path. `interactive` / `hoverStrength` — whether the cursor stirs the dust. `paused` (boolean, false) — freezes the field without unmounting it.
**Motion** — A GPU particle field advanced every frame, with a render-to-texture state pass for pointer stirring, a 2.2 s unfold intro and a 7 s settle. Pauses off-screen through IntersectionObserver and freezes entirely under reduced motion, so a still frame is honest. Needs ogl and WebGL2; no canvas fallback.
**Cost** — ogl@1.0 is a new dependency (~12 KB gzip) plus ~50 KB of component source and four shader passes (field rim, dust, stir, composite); 12k–20k particles with DPR capped by a 4.5-megapixel budget and two float render targets. One canvas, no DOM. Client-only and blank in prerender; on integrated graphics it will keep a laptop fan audible.
**Fit for ARKRETE** — No fit. A glowing orb is the decorative second-colour object the brief rules out, and the home hero is a wordmark, not a bauble.
**Verdict** — skip — heavy GPU ornament with nothing to say about interiors.
