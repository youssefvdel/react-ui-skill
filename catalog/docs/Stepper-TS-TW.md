# Stepper
**Registry**: `Stepper-TS-TW` · **Deps**: @hugeicons/react@^1.1.10, @hugeicons/core-free-icons@^4.3.3 · **Files**: Stepper/Stepper.tsx
**What it does** — A framed multi-step form: a header of numbered tiles, dots or bars with a glider that slides to the active step, one pane visible at a time, and a Back / Continue footer that disappears on the completion pane. Panes slide in from the direction of travel while the container height tweens to fit.
**Props** — `step` / `initialStep` (number, 1) — controlled or uncontrolled; `onStepChange` / `onFinalStepCompleted` — navigation callbacks; `indicator` ('numbers' | 'dots' | 'bars', 'numbers') — header style; `orientation` ('horizontal' | 'vertical', 'horizontal') — rail or row; `transition` ('slide' | 'fade' | 'none', 'slide') — pane change; `linear` (boolean, false) — blocks jumping ahead; `accentColor` (string, theme solid) — glider, badges, next button; `frame` (boolean, true) — the glass panel around everything.
**Motion** — Web Animations API and CSS, no animation library: the glider translates and resizes over 560 ms on a hand-written spring `linear()` curve, the viewport height tweens from its measured old value to the new one, and panes shift 36 px with a 0.25-delayed fade. Reduced motion falls back to fades and instant height.
**Cost** — Hugeicons React plus the icon data for three icons; no motion or GSAP. 27 KB of source, three ResizeObservers, and a step machine that is client state — the first pane prerenders, later panes do not. Default chrome is a translucent dark glass frame with gradient tiles and a solid light next button, all of which needs restyling.
**Fit for ARKRETE** — Contact, if the inquiry is split into three calm steps (the space, the scope, you). The step machinery and height tween are the useful part; the tiles, badges and glass frame are not.
**Verdict** — adapt — keep the step logic and height animation, rebuild the chrome as bone text on black with one thin brass rule; only worth it if the form outgrows a single screen.
