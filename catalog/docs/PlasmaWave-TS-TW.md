# PlasmaWave
**Registry**: `PlasmaWave-TS-TW` · **Deps**: none · **Files**: `PlasmaWave/PlasmaWave.tsx`
**What it does** — A WebGL2 shader of two neon laser ribbons that twist, bend, and undulate through 3D space with an intense HDR glow. Moving the pointer bulges and warps the ribbons through a virtual heat-lens distortion.
**Props** — `colors` (string[], ['#a380ff', '#ff6b9d']) — two-colour palette mapped along the ribbon strands; `speed` / `speed1` / `speed2` (number, 1 / 1 / 1.1) — global and per-strand wave oscillation frequencies; `bend1` / `bend2` (number, 1 / 1.2) — curvature and undulation amplitude for each strand; `thickness` (number, 0.4) — ribbon core radius and cylinder cross-section width; `focalLength` / `rotationDeg` (number, 1.2 / 0) — camera perspective field-of-view and tilt angle; `glow` / `core` (number, 1 / 1) — multi-stage bloom blur strength and white-hot specular intensity; `grain` (number, 0.05) — animated film grain noise overlaid to prevent color banding; `mouseInteraction` (boolean, true) — enables pointer-driven heat swell and lens focal distortion.
**Motion** — Multi-pass WebGL2 pipeline using coarse and fine raymarching passes followed by a 3-level bloom pyramid (downsample and upsample blur passes) and composite pass. Continuous time-based sinusoidal oscillation. Pauses when offscreen and freezes on prefers-reduced-motion.
**Cost** — 24.5 KB source (~4.5 KB gzip), zero dependencies. Single `<canvas>` DOM element. Substantial GPU load with multiple offscreen framebuffers, adaptive raymarching, and bloom pyramid passes every frame. Client-only; blank on static prerender.
**Fit for ARKRETE** — No fit. Glowing neon light tubes and laser bloom belong to nightlife and electronic music visuals, not a quiet Cairo and Dubai fit-out firm.
**Verdict** — skip — multi-pass raymarching and bloom pipeline drawing neon laser ribbons that clash with architectural finishes.
