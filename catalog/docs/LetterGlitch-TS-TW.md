# LetterGlitch
**Registry**: `LetterGlitch-TS-TW` · **Deps**: none · **Files**: `LetterGlitch/LetterGlitch.tsx`
**What it does** — Fills the container with a dense grid of monospace characters rendered from a 2D canvas texture atlas, resting at low opacity and intermittently scrambling glyphs with colored glitch flickers. Moving the mouse stirs a disturbance wake through the letters, and clicking tears or bursts the grid in an expanding shockwave.
**Props** — `characters` (string, DEFAULT_CHARACTERS) — character set for the matrix; `glitchColors` (string[], palette) — momentary glitch highlight colors; `baseColor` (string, '#ffffff' / '#18181b') — resting letter ink; `fontSize` / `spacing` (number, 14 / 0.6) — character size and grid packing; `baseOpacity` / `glitchAmount` (number, 0.16 / 0.5) — resting transparency and glitch frequency; `speed` / `flow` (number, 1 / 0.5) — time rate and directional wave drift; `cursorRadius` / `clickEffect` (number / 'tear' | 'burst' | 'none', 140 / 'tear') — pointer wake radius and click reaction; `smooth` (boolean, true) — whether glitch fade-outs use continuous blending.
**Motion** — WebGL2 instanced character rendering driven by time and pointer interaction buffers on rAF; click ripples trigger decaying physics waves across the grid. No external libraries. Reduced motion halts time progression and click waves, freezing the grid into a static, unscrambled text plane.
**Cost** — Zero dependencies; 35 KB source (~8 KB gzip). Renders thousands of instanced glyphs in WebGL2; moderate GPU draw call load. Client-only; prerenders an empty container.
**Fit for ARKRETE** — no fit. Cyberpunk terminal scrambles and matrix letter glitches directly oppose the bone-and-dark architectural aesthetic.
**Verdict** — skip — cyberpunk ASCII scramble canvas completely at odds with luxury interior photography.
