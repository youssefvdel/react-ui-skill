# CardNav
**Registry**: `CardNav-TS-TW` · **Deps**: @hugeicons/react@^1.1.10, @hugeicons/core-free-icons@^4.3.3 · **Files**: CardNav/CardNav.tsx
**What it does** — A 60px pill bar that springs open into a panel of link cards, the panel's height growing with it. Each card holds a label, one sentence and a short list of links whose arrow swaps for a second arrow on hover.
**Props** — `items` (CardNavItem[], three demo cards) — label, description, optional bg/text colour and links; `cta` ({ label, href }, —) — the button at the bar's right end, repeated full width when narrow; `openOn` ('click' | 'hover', 'click') — what opens it; `cardEffect` ('rise' | 'unfold' | 'fade', 'rise') — how cards arrive; `stagger` (number, 0.12) — seconds between cards; `bounce` (number, 0.2) — spring overshoot on open; `surface` ('glass' | 'solid', 'glass') — backdrop blur or a flat panel; `intro` (boolean, true) — the mount move from a pill to the full bar.
**Motion** — One rAF spring integrator (k = 160·speed², damping from `bounce`) drives a single progress value; panel height, card translate, scale, rotateX and opacity are all read off it, each card offset by `stagger`. The mount intro is a Web Animations `clip-path` wipe plus a staggered rise of the bar's children; the burger and the Menu/Close word swap are CSS. Reduced motion snaps the spring and skips the intro.
**Cost** — 612 lines / 24 KB, no animation library, but the entire 6.4 MB `@hugeicons/core-free-icons` barrel is imported for one arrow icon. Holds 60px of layout permanently while the panel floats over the page. Keyboard-safe: Escape closes and returns focus, links carry `tabIndex={-1}` while shut. Prerenders as a closed bar; the open height is JS-set, so no-JS shows the bar only.
**Fit for ARKRETE** — No fit. Header.tsx opens with a note that the bar must never gain a ground of its own — a slab over a full-bleed photograph is a seam across the picture. This is a floating glass slab, up to 800px wide, over the photograph, and its cards carry the explanatory sentence the site bans.
**Verdict** — skip — it replaces a working groundless header with a panel of marketing blurbs.
