# FadeContent
**Registry**: `FadeContent-TS-TW` · **Deps**: gsap@^3.13.0 · **Files**: FadeContent/FadeContent.tsx
**What it does** — Wraps a block and fades it in when it reaches the viewport, either plainly, up from a blur, or through an SVG turbulence mask that dissolves it as grain, mist or pixel blocks. With `stagger` it animates the block's direct children one after another instead of the block as a whole.
**Props** — `effect` ('fade' | 'focus' | 'develop' | 'dissolve' | 'mist' | 'pixel', 'fade') — the entrance look, of which only fade and focus are clean; `trigger` ('scroll' | 'mount', 'scroll') — viewport entry or immediately; `duration` (number, 1) — seconds, or milliseconds if over 10; `blur` (boolean | number, false) — pixels of defocus on the way in; `stagger` (number, 0) — seconds between direct children; `initialOpacity` (number, 0) — starting alpha; `once` (boolean, true) — stay put after the first pass; `exit` (boolean, false) — fade back out on the way past.
**Motion** — A GSAP timeline driven by ScrollTrigger at `threshold`; the noise effects rebuild a per-element `feTurbulence` mask and step its 33-value alpha table every frame, with an optional blur filter on the same element. Reduced motion skips the tween, sets the block visible and fires `onComplete`.
**Cost** — GSAP core plus ScrollTrigger is roughly 35–45 KB gzip for 14 KB of source. It renders `invisible` until a layout effect runs, so a failed or slow bundle leaves the section blank on a prerendered page. Animated turbulence plus blur over a large section is genuinely GPU-heavy; noise masks on several blocks at once will show.
**Fit for ARKRETE** — Home, the caption under each of the six rooms in the reel; About, the why-it paragraphs.
**Verdict** — adapt — keep the fade and focus path for section entrances, drop the grain/mist/pixel masks, and use `animation-timeline: view()` unless the reel already ships ScrollTrigger.
