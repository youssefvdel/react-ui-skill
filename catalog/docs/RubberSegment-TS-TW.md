# RubberSegment
**Registry**: `RubberSegment-TS-TW` · **Deps**: motion@^12.23.12 · **Files**: RubberSegment/RubberSegment.tsx
**What it does** — A segmented control whose single thumb stretches across the gap between slots and squashes into the target before settling. It is also draggable: the thumb follows the finger, rubber-bands past the ends and projects release velocity, with arrow, Home and End keys handled and a proper radiogroup.
**Props** — `items` (string[] or {value,label,icon}, required) — the segments; `value` / `defaultValue` — controlled or initial selection; `onChange` ((value, index)) — selection callback; `size` ('sm' | 'md' | 'lg', md) — track height and type size; `radius` (10) — corner radius; `stretch` (100%) — how far the thumb dilates mid-travel; `squash` (3px) — overshoot on landing; `draggable` (true) — enables the drag gesture.
**Motion** — Click, keyboard or drag moves a clip-path window over a duplicated row of labels, so the active label recolours as the thumb passes under it. Springs handle dilation, landing and squash; pointer velocity is projected for flicks; reduced motion jumps the edges with no spring. Hover/click/drag triggered, needs motion/react.
**Cost** — motion/react runtime; every item renders twice (a button plus a clipped copy inside the thumb), so labels and fonts must stay in sync. Slots are measured on mount, on resize and after fonts load, and the thumb is zero-width until that first measurement. Client-only for measurement; SSR markup is complete but thumbless until hydration.
**Fit for ARKRETE** — contact, the Cairo/Dubai office switch, the only either/or on the site. Two addresses read better side by side than one hidden behind a toggle, and a squashy thumb is a toy gesture in a brand this quiet.
**Verdict** — skip — a playful control looking for a binary the site does not have, at the cost of the motion runtime.
