# ScrollExpand
**Registry**: `ScrollExpand-TS-TW` · **Deps**: none · **Files**: ScrollExpand/ScrollExpand.tsx
**What it does** — Pins a photograph or video in a sticky stage and grows it from a small rounded plate, 42% × 58% by default, to full bleed as the section scrolls, while a centred title fades out and an optional overlay caption fades in. Crop, corner radius, inner zoom and scroll length are all numbers, so a room can open slowly.
**Props** — `src` (string, required) — image or video path; `mediaType` ('image' | 'video', image) — which element renders; `startWidth` / `startHeight` (42 / 58, %) — the resting plate; `startRadius` / `endRadius` (24 / 0, px) — corners at rest and at full bleed; `mediaZoom` (1.35) — inner image scale before it opens; `scrollDistance` (1.2) — viewport-heights of scroll to full bleed; `holdDistance` (0.35) — extra pinned track after it fills; `useWindowScroll` (false) — drive from the page instead of its own scroller.
**Motion** — Scroll position maps through smoothstep to a clip-path inset, an image scale, and the opacity and offset of title, hint and overlay; an exponential decay filter smooths the value unless smoothing is 0. No library and no rAF once it settles. Reduced motion removes the smoothing lag but keeps the expansion, which is scroll-linked rather than a surprise.
**Cost** — Zero dependencies, 9 KB of source, and only clip-path, transform and opacity on a handful of nodes. Each instance adds a track 2.55× the stage height plus a sticky child; the default own-scroller mode nests a scrollbar-less scroller inside the page, so use `useWindowScroll` for a page-level reel. Prerenders correctly — the start clip is baked into the class — then hydrates to real progress.
**Fit for ARKRETE** — home, the six-room reel: each room arrives as a framed plate and opens to full bleed, driven by window scroll. Also the head of the projects stack. Restyle: endRadius 0, no bold title, no scrim — the caption sits in bone under the frame.
**Verdict** — adapt — the clip-path expansion is the reel's missing move and costs nothing; take the mechanism, drop the scrim, bold title and rounded corners.
