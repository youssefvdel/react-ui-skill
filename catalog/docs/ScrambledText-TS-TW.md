# ScrambledText
**Registry**: `ScrambledText-TS-TW` · **Deps**: gsap@^3.13.0 · **Files**: ScrambledText/ScrambledText.tsx
**What it does** — Splits a paragraph into characters and, when the cursor comes within a radius, makes nearby glyphs churn through `.:` before resolving into the real letter. Clicking sends a wave across the text that scrambles every glyph it crosses; lift, blur and magnify variants shove the letters physically instead.
**Props** — `effect` ('scramble' | 'lift' | 'blur' | 'magnify', scramble) — what the cursor does to glyphs; `radius` (100px) — reach around the pointer; `duration` (1.2s) — decode length; `speed` (0.5) — churn rate; `scrambleChars` (':.') — substitute glyphs; `clickBurst` (true) — click sends a wave; `intro` (true) — decodes once on first view; `strength` (1) — displacement amount for physical effects.
**Motion** — Hover proximity, click waves and a one-time decode on first intersection. Each affected glyph runs a GSAP ScrambleText tween, with duration scaled by distance; the physical variants run on a rAF that stops once energy decays. Reduced motion drops the intro and the displacement but still scrambles glyphs on hover. Needs GSAP SplitText and ScrambleTextPlugin.
**Cost** — GSAP plus SplitText and ScrambleTextPlugin — core alone is ~25 KB gzip, plugins on top; both plugins ship in the public gsap package from 3.13. Every character becomes a span holding a hidden sizer plus an absolutely positioned glyph, and positions are re-measured by walking the offsetParent chain per character. Waits on `document.fonts.ready`, so it re-splits after webfonts land. Client-only; prerender shows the plain paragraph.
**Fit for ARKRETE** — no fit. Glyph decryption is a terminal trope, the wrong register for interiors, and it imposes its own mono font and 7vw margins.
**Verdict** — skip — a hacker effect that fights both the typography and the calm the brand needs, for a heavy new plugin dependency.
