# BounceCards
**Registry**: `BounceCards-TS-TW` · **Deps**: none · **Files**: BounceCards/BounceCards.tsx
**What it does** — Deals a handful of square photo cards into an overlapping fan, popping them in one at a time, then springs them apart on hover so the chosen card straightens and rises while its neighbours are pushed aside. Clicking a card gives it a downward kick and fires a callback.
**Props** — `images` (string[] or `{src, alt}[]`, required) — the photos; `cardSize` (number, 200) — card edge in px; `spread` (number, 85) — horizontal gap between fan slots; `rotation` (number, 10) — maximum tilt in degrees; `arc` (number, 0) — vertical bow of the fan in px; `bounciness` (number, 0.6) — spring overshoot on entry; `hoverScale` (number, 1.06) — lift of the hovered card; `onCardClick` (function) — makes cards focusable buttons and reports the index.
**Motion** — A single hand-written spring integrator on rAF, substepped at 240 Hz, writing `translate3d`/`rotate`/`scale` straight to each card and sleeping when the fan settles. Hover is picked by nearest slot; click adds a velocity impulse. Reduced motion snaps scale to 1, stiffens movement and cancels the pop. No library.
**Cost** — No dependencies, about 3.7 KB gzipped source, one div and one img per card, transforms only. The canvas is a fixed 400×400 by default, so it needs a measured wrapper to be responsive. Cards are authored at `scale(0)` and only revealed by JS — nothing appears without hydration, so the fan cannot be server-rendered content. Hover spread needs a fine pointer; touch gets the click but not the fan-out.
**Fit for ARKRETE** — About, the "its trades" section: five cards as material samples that sit fanned on the table and open on hover, each linking into services.
**Verdict** — adapt — cheap and tactile, but only with 4:5 crops at 300px+, a hairline bone border instead of 5px white, the fan as the resting state, and a static fan for touch.
