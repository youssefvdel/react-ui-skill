# DodgeField
**Registry**: `DodgeField-TS-TW` · **Deps**: motion@^12.23.12 · **Files**: DodgeField/DodgeField.tsx
**What it does** — A fixed-height field holding one child that slides away from the pointer, further the closer the pointer gets, until it relents after `patience` dodges and lets itself be clicked. A default button cycles taunts ("Catch me", "Nope", "Too slow") and inverts for a moment when caught.
**Props** — `children` (node | (state) => node, a taunt button) — what flees, or a render prop with dodges / gave / caught / fleeing; `taunts` (string[], five lines) — the label cycle; `reach` (number, 72) — px of travel at full proximity; `radius` (number, 120) — px at which it notices the pointer; `patience` (number, 4) — dodges before it gives up; `axis` ('both' | 'x' | 'y', 'both') — escape direction; `wall` ('clamp' | 'bounce', 'clamp') — what happens at the field edge; `notice` (string, '') — line shown on touch devices.
**Motion** — Framer Motion: two motion values in a `useMotionTemplate` translate, retargeted by `animate()` springs on every pointer move — 130ms out, 620ms home with 0.1 bounce. Pointer position is read on `pointermove` and turned into one rAF; a ResizeObserver keeps the field's half-room. Reduced motion pins it at zero; coarse pointers and touch get the still child.
**Cost** — `motion` measures 45 KB gzip for the published package, and `motion.div`, `animate`, `useMotionValue` and `useReducedMotion` are most of what it is. 302 lines / 10 KB. It listens on `window` for pointermove, scroll and blur for the field's whole life, at page level rather than on itself. Prerenders fine — the child is in the markup, the transform starts at zero.
**Fit for ARKRETE** — No fit. Every path on this site ends at "Request a proposal"; a button that runs from the client's cursor is the opposite of the firm's job, and the taunts are a startup voice, not a Cairo fit-out contractor's.
**Verdict** — skip — 45 KB of Framer Motion for a gag with no page it can live on.
