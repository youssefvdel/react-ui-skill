# ScrollReveal
**Registry**: `ScrollReveal-TS-TW` · **Deps**: gsap@^3.13.0 · **Files**: ScrollReveal/ScrollReveal.tsx
**What it does** — Splits a paragraph into words, characters or lines and brings them up from a dim, slightly blurred, three-degree-rotated state to full opacity as the block climbs the viewport. In scrub mode the words are tied to the scrollbar; in enter mode the block plays once when it arrives.
**Props** — `mode` ('scrub' | 'enter', scrub) — scroll-linked or play once; `splitBy` ('words' | 'chars' | 'lines', words) — reveal unit; `effect` ('fade' | 'rise' | 'flip' | 'scale', fade) — how a unit lands; `baseOpacity` (0.1) — where unrevealed words sit; `baseRotation` (3deg) — tilt of the block, straightened over the same scroll; `blurStrength` (4px) — starting blur; `stagger` (0.05s) — gap between units; `wordAnimationEnd` ('bottom bottom') — when the last word lands.
**Motion** — Scroll progress becomes a per-unit t, and each word gets opacity, translate, blur and optional colour from it; the block itself rotates from baseRotation to zero across the same trigger. GSAP and ScrollTrigger under the standard no-preference matchMedia gate, painting opacity, transform and filter only, with nothing running when idle. Reduced motion leaves the text fully opaque and unrotated.
**Cost** — GSAP core plus ScrollTrigger, ~35 KB gzip, so it must be code-split to the routes that use it. One span per word, or per character if split, so a 60-word paragraph is 60+ nodes; will-change is dropped above 80 pieces. Prerender is clean — the full paragraph ships in the HTML, unanimated, which is what a static page needs.
**Fit for ARKRETE** — about, the statement paragraph under the firm's name: words settle as you read and the block straightens from 2°. Also the project captions. Use `mode="enter"`, `baseRotation={2}`, `blurStrength={2}` and leave highlightColor empty.
**Verdict** — adapt — restrained and readable, the one effect here that adds pace to the copy rather than to the photography.
