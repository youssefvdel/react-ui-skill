# Dither
**Registry**: `Dither-TS-TW` · **Deps**: none · **Files**: `Dither/Dither.tsx`
**What it does** — A full-bleed field of slow noise waves rendered as a Bayer, halftone, line or crosshatch dither, in up to eight palette steps. The pointer erases, reveals or ripples the pattern as it crosses the surface.
**Props** — `ditherMode` (`'bayer' | 'bayer4' | 'bayer2' | 'halftone' | 'lines' | 'crosshatch' | 'noise' | 'none'`, `'bayer'`) — dither matrix · `palette` (string[], max 8) — replaces the colour ramp · `pixelSize` (number, `2`) — cell size · `colorNum` (number, `4`) — colour steps · `waveColor` / `backgroundColor` (string or RGB triple) — the two ends · `waveSpeed` (number, `0.05`) / `waveFrequency` (number, `3`) / `waveAmplitude` (number, `0.3`) — the field · `mouseEffect` (`'erase' | 'reveal' | 'ripple'`, `'erase'`) — pointer behaviour · `intro` (boolean, `true`) — reveal on mount.
**Motion** — Two WebGL2 passes: an fbm wave field drawn into a half-resolution framebuffer, then a dither pass sampled at cell resolution. Pointer positions feed a 16-point trail and up to four click ripples that warp the field. The loop stops when idle, offscreen or the tab is hidden; reduced motion freezes the clock but still draws one frame.
**Cost** — No dependencies, 24.8 KB source (7.3 KB gzip): two shader programs and a framebuffer. It fills its parent (`h-full w-full`), so it needs an explicitly sized box, and it adds window-level `pointermove` and `pointerdown` listeners plus a DPR cap of 2. Loses the GL context on unmount. Client-only.
**Fit for ARKRETE** — No fit. This is ambient shader behind content; the site's ground should stay a still near-black so the photography is the only texture on the page.
**Verdict** — skip — two-pass GPU decoration with no section to carry.
