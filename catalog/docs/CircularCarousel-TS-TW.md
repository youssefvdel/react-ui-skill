# CircularCarousel
**Registry**: `CircularCarousel-TS-TW` · **Deps**: none · **Files**: CircularCarousel/CircularCarousel.tsx
**What it does** — Renders an interactive ring of images arranged in 3D cylindrical, orbital, or panorama layouts with inertia dragging and click-to-focus. Cards bend along the curve by subdividing their geometry into eight segmented DOM slices.
**Props** — `items` (CircularCarouselItem[], 10 demo items) — image array with titles and descriptions; `preset` ('cylinder' | 'orbit' | 'wheel' | 'panorama', 'cylinder') — preset 3D layout geometry; `curve` (number, 1) — cylindrical arc curvature factor; `tilt` (number, -5) — vertical tilt angle in degrees; `draggable` (boolean, true) — enables pointer drag gestures; `snap` (boolean, true) — magnetic snapping to nearest card; `autoplay` ('drift' | 'step' | 'off', 'off') — automatic rotation mode; `depthFade` (number, 0) — opacity attenuation for background cards.
**Motion** — Custom rAF spring loop (stiffness 118, settle speed 9) calculating 3D rotation, drag momentum, and subtle cursor tilt parallax. Each card's 8 DOM slices receive calculated `translate3d` and `rotateY` transforms. Reduced motion disables intro animations, lerp, and pointer parallax. No external motion libraries.
**Cost** — Zero dependencies, but 38 KB source and extreme DOM footprint (8 slice divs per card × N cards) causing continuous 3D compositing strain. Safe for static prerender but visually flat until hydrated.
**Fit for ARKRETE** — no fit. Slicing interior photography into eight faceted DOM strips warps and fragments architectural lines; Arkrete's photography requires undistorted full-bleed presentation.
**Verdict** — skip — bending photography via eight sliced DOM strips distorts architectural lines and creates severe compositing overhead.
