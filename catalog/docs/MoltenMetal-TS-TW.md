# MoltenMetal
**Registry**: `MoltenMetal-TS-TW` · **Deps**: none · **Files**: MoltenMetal/MoltenMetal.tsx
**What it does** — Renders swirling caustic filament tendrils and glowing liquid-metal ribbons across a full canvas via a WebGL2 shader. As the cursor drags across the screen, it leaves a burning thermal trail of heightened brightness and heat, and clicks produce expanding incendiary flares.
**Props** — `colors` (string[], ['#5227FF', '#FF9FFC']) — palette array interpolated across caustic contours; `coreColor` (string, '#FFFFFF') — hottest filament core color; `speed` (number, 0.35) — evolution speed of the procedural turbulence field; `scale` (number, 4) — zoom level of the procedural noise filaments; `glow` (number, 1.6) — bloom intensity around filament edges; `mouseInteraction` (boolean, true) — whether cursor movement leaves thermal heat trails; `flareOnClick` (boolean, true) — whether clicking triggers expanding fiery burst rings; `colorMode` ('molten' | 'ember' | 'frost', 'molten') — palette balance profile.
**Motion** — WebGL2 shader on requestAnimationFrame running procedural fbm noise, domain warping, thermal trail decay, and click flare expansions. Mousemove appends points to a decaying heat trail queue. Click triggers time-based flare objects. No GSAP, Framer Motion, or external 3D libraries. Reads `prefers-reduced-motion`: the simulation clock stops, pointer leaning is off and click flares are suppressed, so it renders a still frame.
**Cost** — 649 lines (~22 KB source), zero external dependencies. Heavy per-pixel procedural noise and multi-point trail calculation in WebGL2 fragment shader (high GPU fill-rate cost, mobile battery draw). Client-only: SSR renders placeholder div and canvas, shader initializes in useEffect.
**Fit for ARKRETE** — no fit. The caustic incandescent neon lava/plasma aesthetic is diametrically opposed to ARKRETE's palette of near-black ground, bone ink, and quiet architectural materials; fiery trails and click flares are video-game distractions with no editorial purpose.
**Verdict** — skip — incandescent caustic lava effect with fiery cursor trails that directly contradicts the dark architectural tone.
