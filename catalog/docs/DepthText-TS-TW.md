# DepthText
**Registry**: `DepthText-TS-TW` · **Deps**: none · **Files**: `DepthText/DepthText.tsx`
**What it does** — A word is drawn as 34 stacked copies of itself, each pushed a couple of pixels further back, so it reads as solid extruded type. It leans toward the pointer, drifts on its own when left alone, and grows out of flat type on first paint.
**Props** — `text` (string, `'Elevate'`) — the word · `layers` (number 2–64, `34`) — depth slices, the DOM cost · `depth` (number, `2.4`) — px between slices · `faceColor` (string, `currentColor`) / `depthColor` (string, `'#7c3aed'`) — front and extrusion · `tilt` (number 0–12, `7.5`) — how far it leans · `pointerTracking` (boolean, `true`) — follow the cursor · `autoOrbit` (boolean, `true`) / `orbitSpeed` (number, `0.35`) — idle sweep · `hoverPush` (number 0–1, `0`) — sink on hover or press.
**Motion** — A CSS `perspective` wrapper holds a `preserve-3d` stage; each slice sits at `translateZ(-n × depth)` and the stack's rotateX/rotateY is eased toward the pointer, or swept by the orbit when the pointer is away. A spring sinks the stack on hover and press, and the intro grows the extrusion from zero. Reduced motion returns early: static stack, no listeners, no rAF.
**Cost** — No dependencies, 13.7 KB source (4.0 KB gzip). Default 34 copies of the word are absolutely positioned text nodes, so DOM count scales directly with `layers` — cap it at eight or ten. `autoOrbit` keeps a rAF alive indefinitely; turn it off and the loop parks itself once settled. The real word is in the DOM, so it prerenders and is readable before hydration.
**Fit for ARKRETE** — Home, on the monument wordmark: six to ten layers, `depth` near 0.8, `autoOrbit` off, `depthColor` a bone-on-black step, `tilt` under 4.
**Verdict** — adapt — the only component here that deepens the wordmark without a dependency, once the slice count and the orbit are cut.
